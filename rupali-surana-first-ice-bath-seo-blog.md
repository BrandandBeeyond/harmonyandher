## Safety Comes Before Intensity

A professionally facilitated ice bath should never be approached as a test of endurance.

Before participating, individuals should disclose relevant health conditions, follow the facilitator’s preparation instructions and obtain medical guidance when appropriate. People with heart or circulatory conditions, uncontrolled blood pressure, pregnancy, a history of fainting or other health concerns should consult a qualified medical professional before attempting cold-water immersion.

Participants must be free to stop at any point. The purpose is conscious exploration, not forced endurance.
